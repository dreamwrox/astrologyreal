# Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) — Prediction Method Reference

This reference encodes the BNN method for the AstroBot prompt. It is a restatement
of the technique in our own words for the model to apply. The method here follows
the system popularized by R.G. Rao and taught in introductory BNN texts.

## Core principles that make BNN different
- BNN does NOT use the Lagna (Ascendant) or Dasha periods. These depend on an exact
  birth time, which is often unknown, so BNN avoids them entirely.
- Jupiter is treated as the SELF (Jeeva karaka / life-force). Wherever Jupiter sits is
  read as the 1st house, and the other 11 houses are counted anticlockwise from there.
- Prediction is done by JOINING THE KARAKAS (significations) of planets that are "in
  combination." This karaka-joining is the engine of every BNN reading.

## Planet karakas (significations)
- SUN (Surya): government, politics, administration, government job, power, status,
  name, fame, top position, ego, dominance; father; male child (son); right eye; bones;
  immunity; the soul/Atma.
- MOON (Chandra): change of all kinds (job change, city change, house change, transfer,
  travel); the mind; mother; elder daughter; food; water; emotions.
- MARS (Mangal): energy and drive; younger brother; the HUSBAND in a female chart (so
  marriage questions in a female chart are read from Mars); land/property; surgery;
  accidents; blood; sharp objects; engineering/machinery/technical work.
- MERCURY (Budh): intelligence, knowledge, memory, education; youngest sibling; friends,
  relatives, neighbours; boyfriend/girlfriend; business; communication; area of interest.
- JUPITER (Guru): the SELF (Jeeva karaka) — represents the native in both male and female
  charts; religion/God; teacher, guide, judge, manager, lawyer, accountant, consultant,
  banker; honour, respect, truth, patience, humility.
- VENUS (Shukra): beauty, charm, attraction; money, luxury, house, vehicles, garments,
  diamonds; the WIFE in a male chart; desire; (karaka of BNN study itself).
- SATURN (Shani): career/profession (karma karaka) — a career signification must connect
  to Saturn to manifest; delay, obstacles, procrastination, hurdles; cold/dark places;
  discipline; laziness/lethargy.
- RAHU: foreign countries, different culture/caste, MNC, export, online/software, Tarot;
  expansion (it magnifies what it joins); desire/attachment/Maya; disease (Kaal);
  hallucination; paternal grandparents.
- KETU: detachment; yogi/sanyasi; the occult, astrology, meditation, yoga, coding,
  research, acupressure; endings; long/cylindrical things; tensions/dejection; moksha;
  maternal grandparents.

## How planets come "into combination"
Two or more planets are read together (in combination/conjunction) if ANY of these hold:
1. They sit in the SAME rashi.
2. They sit in TRINAL rashis (forming a triangle). The four trinal groups are:
   - 1-5-9  (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
   - 2-6-10 (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
   - 3-7-11 (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)
   - 4-8-12 (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
3. One planet is in the NEXT rashi to another (e.g. a planet in Pisces/12 combines with a
   planet in Aries/1).
4. One planet is in the 7TH rashi from another (counting 7 signs away).
   EXCEPTION: for RAHU and KETU, the 7th-house combination is NOT used.

## The prediction method
To read any combination, simply JOIN the karakas of the planets involved and state the
plain-language result. Example logic: Saturn (career) + Moon (change) = frequent job
changes or a transferable/travelling job.

## Worked combination meanings from the book (apply the same joining logic for others)
- JUPITER + SUN: native resembles father; promises a male child; interest in politics,
  desire for name/fame/respect; leadership and a "kingly" nature; spiritual with strong
  intuition. (Self + father/soul/government.)
- SATURN + MOON: many changes in profession; transferable/travelling job or frequent job
  changes; suited to food, water, travel, numerology, nursing, hotel management,
  psychology, economics; tendency to cough/cold/asthma and to feel low/depressed;
  mother's life burdensome. (Career + change.)
- VENUS + RAHU: "Bahu Dhan yoga" — much money, big vehicle, large house; in a male chart,
  love marriage across culture/caste or foreign travel after marriage; spouse may do
  Rahu-type work (software, Tarot, online, MNC); flirty nature. (Money + expansion/foreign.)
- MARS + KETU: in a FEMALE chart, marital disputes/separation, in extreme cases divorce
  (Mars = husband, Ketu = separation). Also "Hanuman yoga." NEVER declare divorce certain
  without seeing the spouse's chart; a saving combination (e.g. Jupiter+Venus) may offset it.
- MERCURY + SATURN: "Kumbhkaran yoga" — heavy procrastination, last-minute work, low
  interest in study yet good last-day marks; career needing communication; with Sun also
  linked to Saturn, MBA-type path. (Intelligence/education + delay/career.)
- MERCURY + MOON: "elephant memory"; changes in school/subjects; psychology/numerology;
  food or tours-and-travel business. Also "Kapat yoga" — risk of being cheated; money lent
  may not return; partnerships can lead to cheating. (Intelligence/memory + mind/change.)
- VENUS + MOON: money flows like water, poor savings; trading/stock losses likely; in a
  female chart, friction with mother-in-law and gynaecological issues. Also "Annapurna
  yoga." (Money + water/loss/change.)

## Jupiter through the 12 rashis (the self takes on that sign's traits)
- Aries: shorter height (unless benefic aspect), hard struggle, sporty, aggressive,
  stubborn, frugal; real estate/technical/archeology/geology interests.
- Taurus: good build, good-looking, money-minded and expense-watching, soft-spoken.
- Gemini: good-looking, tall, dual personality, hard to read, media/writing/oratory,
  good traveller, argumentative; twins may appear in the family.
- Cancer (exalted): open-hearted, dislikes lies, soft-spoken, settles in many places,
  likes sweets and travel.
- Leo: strong dominating personality, good build, broad shoulders, shining face.
- Virgo: looks soft but very stubborn, persuasive/manipulative, self-assured, good
  finances, shines in media or fine arts.
- Libra: doesn't take things personally; in a male chart womanizing tendencies/many female
  friends; money-minded; fine arts; good mediator.
- Scorpio: complicated, torn between material and spiritual, empathetic, soft-hearted.
- Sagittarius: detached from worldly life, wanderer, knowledge-sharer, self-taught, turns
  spiritual, believes in cosmic energy.
- Capricorn (debilitated but workable): well-behaved, popular locally, witty, leads others,
  recognition, from a good family, materially attached, wisdom-oriented.
- Aquarius (not ideal): psychic, unstable, non-judgmental, fast thinking, unpredictable,
  self-earning; mood swings disrupt life.
- Pisces (good): self-made, serves the poor and aged, charitable to temples/old-age
  homes/orphanages, golden-hearted, moksha-seeking, loses interest in material life.

## Tone and honesty rules for readings
- Always JOIN the karakas explicitly so the logic is visible (e.g. "Saturn is career and
  Moon is change, so...").
- Never declare an extreme life event (divorce, serious illness) as certain. The book itself
  warns against this — note that a single combination is not the whole chart and offsetting
  combinations may exist.
- Frame readings as BNN-based guidance, not guaranteed fact. Avoid "100% certain" language
  even though the book uses dramatic phrasing.
- BNN reads an existing kundli (planet-in-rashi placements). If the app does not yet compute
  the real chart, ask the user for their planetary placements, or generate readings from the
  sign/birth details available and say the reading is indicative.
